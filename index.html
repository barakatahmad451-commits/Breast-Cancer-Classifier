🔬 Clinical Interpretation Guide

Higher measurements in the selected tumor metrics generally correlate with greater surface irregularity and increased malignancy risk.

  • Concave Points: Quantify boundary indentations and morphologic irregularity
  • Perimeter Measurements: Identify irregular tumor shape and boundary complexity
  • Radius & Area: Capture tumor size, volume, and growth progression
  • Statistical Validation: All features independently selected for predictive power
